    RSVP questions

    Ask guests a little more

    Dietary needs, plus-one names, song requests — anything you like. Asked on the RSVP form, only of guests who are coming. Answers show up in your guest list and export.

    Weather

    Show the forecast on your event page

    Pick the town your event is in and guests see the weather for that day. Near the date it's a real forecast; further out it says what that week is usually like. This is separate from the address you wrote for guests — it's only ever as precise as a town.
